## Quick Definition

Jazz is a music genre that originated in the African-American communities of New Orleans in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. It is characterized by swing, blue notes, complex chords, call and response vocals, polyrhythms, and improvisation.

## Core Definition

Jazz is a music genre that originated in the African-American communities of New Orleans, Louisiana, in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Its roots are in blues, ragtime, European harmony, African rhythmic rituals, spirituals, hymns, marches, vaudeville song, and dance music ([jazz](/source/jazz/)). Since the 1920s Jazz Age, it has been recognized as a major form of musical expression in traditional and popular music. Jazz is characterized by swing and blue notes, complex chords, call and response vocals, polyrhythms and improvisation ([jazz](/source/jazz/)).

## Key Properties

1. Originated in African-American communities of New Orleans in the late 19th/early 20th century
2. Roots in blues, ragtime, European harmony, and African rhythmic traditions
3. Characterized by swing feel, blue notes, complex chords, and improvisation
4. Features call and response patterns and polyrhythms
5. Has spawned numerous subgenres: bebop, cool jazz, hard bop, modal jazz, free jazz, fusion
6. Drew on national, regional, and local musical cultures as it spread worldwide

## Context & Application

Jazz evolved through a series of stylistic innovations over the 20th century. New Orleans jazz began in the early 1910s; the 1930s saw the rise of swing big bands; bebop emerged in the 1940s as a more complex "musician's music"; cool jazz developed near the end of the 1940s with calmer, smoother sounds; modal jazz and free jazz emerged in the late 1950s; and jazz fusion combined jazz improvisation with rock rhythms in the late 1960s ([jazz](/source/jazz/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([jazz](/source/jazz/)): New Orleans jazz combined brass band marches, French quadrilles, ragtime and blues with collective polyphonic improvisation in the early 1910s.

**Example 2** ([jazz](/source/jazz/)): Bebop emerged in the 1940s, shifting jazz from danceable popular music toward a more challenging style played at faster tempos with chord-based improvisation.

## Relationships

- **cool-jazz** -- Cool jazz is a subgenre that developed calmer, smoother sounds in contrast to bebop
- **bebop** -- Bebop was a pivotal jazz subgenre that shifted the genre toward greater complexity
- **modal-jazz** -- Modal jazz used modes rather than chord changes as the basis for improvisation
- **improvisation** -- Improvisation is central to jazz performance practice
- **blues** -- Blues is one of the primary roots of jazz
