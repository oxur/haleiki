## Quick Definition

A brane is a physical object in string theory that generalizes the concept of a point particle (0-brane) and a string (1-brane) to higher dimensions. A $p$-brane is a $p$-dimensional object that sweeps out a ($p$+1)-dimensional worldvolume as it propagates through spacetime.

## Core Definition

In string theory and related theories, a brane is a physical object that generalizes the notion of a point particle to higher dimensions. A point particle is a 0-brane, a string is a 1-brane, and it is possible to consider higher-dimensional branes. In dimension $p$, these are called $p$-branes. Branes are dynamical objects that can propagate through spacetime according to quantum mechanics, have mass and charge, and sweep out ($p$+1)-dimensional worldvolumes. The word "brane" comes from "membrane," which refers to a 2-brane ([string-theory](/source/string-theory/)). In M-theory, the fundamental objects include two-dimensional branes (membranes) and five-dimensional branes (fivebranes) ([m-theory](/source/m-theory/)).

## Prerequisites

- **String theory** -- branes arise within the framework of string theory and its extensions.

## Key Properties

1. **Generalized dimension** -- a $p$-brane has $p$ spatial dimensions ([string-theory](/source/string-theory/)).
2. **Dynamical** -- branes propagate and interact according to quantum mechanics ([m-theory](/source/m-theory/)).
3. **Worldvolume** -- a $p$-brane sweeps out a ($p$+1)-dimensional volume in spacetime ([string-theory](/source/string-theory/)).
4. **Charges** -- branes can carry charges analogous to electric charge ([m-theory](/source/m-theory/)).
5. **Central to M-theory** -- M-theory describes 2-branes and 5-branes in eleven dimensions ([m-theory](/source/m-theory/)).

## Context & Application

Branes were first considered by Dirac in 1962 and became central to string theory in the late 1980s-1990s. The discovery that eleven-dimensional supergravity includes 2-branes (1987) and that wrapped membranes reproduce type IIA strings was a key step toward M-theory. D-branes, a special class where open string endpoints must lie, led to the Strominger-Vafa black hole entropy calculation and the AdS/CFT correspondence ([string-theory](/source/string-theory/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([m-theory](/source/m-theory/)): A 2-brane (membrane) in eleven-dimensional supergravity, when wrapped around a compact circular dimension, reproduces exactly the strings of type IIA superstring theory.

**Example 2** ([string-theory](/source/string-theory/)): D-branes in string theory are surfaces where open string endpoints must lie, and the study of D-brane systems led to the derivation of black hole entropy.
