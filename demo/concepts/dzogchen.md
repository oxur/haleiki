## Quick Definition

Dzogchen ("Great Perfection") is the highest teaching in the Nyingma school of Tibetan Buddhism, concerned with the direct recognition of intrinsic awareness (rigpa) as the fundamental nature of mind. It is transmitted through lineages traced back to Garab Dorje through Padmasambhava and was systematized by Longchenpa.

## Core Definition

The Nyingma school traditionally holds that its Dzogchen lineage has its origins in Garab Dorje through a lineage of transmission to Padmasambhava ([padmasambhava](/source/padmasambhava/)). Nyangral Nyima Ozer (1124-1192) "first linked Padmasambhava to the Great Perfection in a high-profile manner" ([padmasambhava](/source/padmasambhava/)). Longchenpa wrote extensively on Dzogchen and systematized its teachings: his Seven Treasuries were written to elucidate the extraordinarily profound meaning of the seventeen main tantras of Dzogchen ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)). As one of the eight manifestations of Padmasambhava, Guru Nyima Ozer "illuminates the darkness of the mind through the insight of Dzogchen" ([padmasambhava](/source/padmasambhava/)).

## Prerequisites

- **Nyingma** -- Dzogchen is the highest teaching within the Nyingma school; understanding the Nyingma tradition provides the necessary context of lineage, transmission, and practice framework.

## Key Properties

1. **Highest teaching** -- considered the pinnacle of the nine yanas (vehicles) in the Nyingma classification system.
2. **Direct recognition** -- focuses on the direct recognition of the nature of mind (rigpa, intrinsic awareness) rather than gradual cultivation.
3. **Lineage from Garab Dorje** -- the Dzogchen lineage is traced from Garab Dorje through Padmasambhava ([padmasambhava](/source/padmasambhava/)).
4. **Systematized by Longchenpa** -- Longchenpa brought together the Vima Nyingtik and Khandro Nyingtik teachings, writing the Three Yangtik (Inner Essences) and the Seven Treasuries to elucidate Dzogchen ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)).
5. **Seventeen tantras** -- the foundational texts of Dzogchen are the seventeen main tantras of Dzogpachenpo.
6. **Cross-school interest** -- Dzogchen teachings are not confined to the Nyingma; the hidden lake temple of the Dalai Lamas (Lukhang) is dedicated to Dzogchen teachings ([padmasambhava](/source/padmasambhava/)).

## Context & Application

The Seven Line Prayer to Padmasambhava is said to contain the most sacred and important teachings of Dzogchen ([padmasambhava](/source/padmasambhava/)). Longchenpa gathered his own termas and those of earlier masters in the thirteen-volume Nyingtik Yabshi, which is the practice aspect of his writings and the basis of the Old Nyingtik. In it he synthesized the Vima Nyingtik of Vimalamitra and the Khandro Nyingtik of Guru Rinpoche ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)). Longchenpa transmitted the Longchen Nyingtik cycle of teachings and practice to Jikme Lingpa, and it has since become one of the most widely practiced traditions ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)): Longchenpa's Seven Treasuries were specifically written to elucidate the meaning of the seventeen main tantras of Dzogchen and the teachings of all nine yanas.

**Example 2** ([padmasambhava](/source/padmasambhava/)): The Lukhang, the hidden lake temple of the Dalai Lamas behind the Potala Palace, is dedicated to Dzogchen teachings and has murals depicting the eight manifestations of Padmasambhava.
