## Quick Definition

The Longchen Nyingtik ("Heart Essence of the Vast Expanse") is a Nyingma cycle of Dzogchen teachings and practice discovered by Jikme Lingpa as mind terma in 1757, constituting a complete path to enlightenment from preliminary practices through Dzogchen.

## Core Definition

The Longchen Nyingtik is a Nyingma cycle of teachings and practice discovered by Jikme Lingpa as mind terma ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)). Guru Rinpoche (Padmasambhava) conferred the Longchen Nyingtik teachings on King Trisong Detsen, Khandro Yeshe Tsogyal, and Vairochana, giving prophetic empowerments that the teachings would be discovered by Jikme Lingpa, an incarnation of King Trisong Detsen ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)). Jikme Lingpa discovered these teachings at the age of twenty-eight in 1757 through a visionary experience in which wisdom dakinis gave him a casket containing five yellow scrolls and seven crystal beads ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/), [jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)). It has since become one of the most widely practiced Dzogchen traditions ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).

- **Dzogchen** -- the Longchen Nyingtik is a Dzogchen practice cycle; familiarity with Dzogchen is essential.

## Key Properties

1. **Mind terma** -- discovered through visionary experience rather than physical discovery ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)).
2. **Complete path** -- includes preliminary and main practices, development and completion stages, and Dzogchen/Atiyoga, constituting a complete path to enlightenment ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)).
3. **Link to Longchenpa** -- Jikme Lingpa's mind became one with the wisdom mind of Longchenpa through three visions during retreat ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)).
4. **Innermost Nyingtik** -- the Nyingtik teachings are the innermost secret teachings of Dzogchen, the most direct approach for students of the highest capacity ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)).
5. **Most widely practiced** -- became one of the most widely used Nyingma Dzogchen teachings ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).

## Context & Application

Jikme Lingpa kept this terma secret for years and did not transcribe it until entering another retreat at Chimpu near Samye monastery, where he had three visions of Longchenpa. On the tenth day of the monkey month of 1764, he made the terma public by conferring empowerment and instructions upon fifteen disciples ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)). The Longchen Nyingtik lineage includes both the terma discovered by Jikme Lingpa and teachings of Longchenpa on Vima Nyingtik and Khandro Nyingtik that were revealed to Jikme Lingpa in visions ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)): In 1757, Jikme Lingpa experienced flying through the sky riding a white lion to Boudhanath Stupa, where wisdom dakinis gave him five yellow scrolls and seven crystal beads. At a dakini's instruction, he ate them, and all the words and meaning of the Longchen Nyingtik terma were awakened in his mind.
