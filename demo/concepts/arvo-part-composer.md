## Quick Definition

Arvo Part (b. 1935) is an Estonian composer known for developing the tintinnabuli compositional technique, which combines a melodic voice with a tintinnabuli voice that arpeggiates a triad. He is one of the most performed living composers of classical music.

## Core Definition

Arvo Part is an Estonian composer born in Paide in 1935 ([arvo-part](/source/arvo-part/)). He is known for developing the tintinnabuli technique, a distinctive compositional method characterized by the pairing of a stepwise-moving melodic voice with a triadic "tintinnabuli" voice (from the Latin for "bells"). His music is often associated with sacred minimalism, drawing on both minimalist reduction and deep spiritual devotion. Part studied at the Estonian Academy of Music and Theatre and has been one of the most widely performed living composers of classical music.

## Context & Application

Part's tintinnabuli style emerged after a period of creative crisis in the early 1970s, during which he studied Gregorian chant, Renaissance polyphony, and the music of the early music period. The resulting technique strips music to its essential elements -- a melody and a triad -- creating music of stark, bell-like clarity ([arvo-part](/source/arvo-part/)).
