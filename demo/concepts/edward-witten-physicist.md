## Quick Definition

Edward Witten (born 1951) is an American mathematical physicist at the Institute for Advanced Study, widely regarded as the most influential theoretical physicist of his generation. He is the only physicist to have won the Fields Medal (1990) and is known for proposing M-theory and developing topological quantum field theory.

## Core Definition

Edward Witten is an American theoretical physicist born August 26, 1951, in Baltimore, Maryland. He received his BA from Brandeis University and his PhD from Princeton University. He is a professor at the Institute for Advanced Study. Witten is known for M-theory, Seiberg-Witten theory, topological quantum field theory, the Wess-Zumino-Witten model, the Gromov-Witten invariant, the positive energy theorem, and numerous other contributions to mathematical physics ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)).

## Key Properties

1. **Fields Medal** -- the only physicist to have won the Fields Medal (1990), for work on knot invariants and topological quantum field theory ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)).
2. **M-theory** -- proposed in 1995 that the five superstring theories are limiting cases of a single eleven-dimensional theory ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)).
3. **TQFT** -- introduced Witten-type topological quantum field theories, connecting physics to knot theory and four-manifold invariants ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)).
4. **Prolific contributions** -- known for the Seiberg-Witten invariants, Chern-Simons theory applications, twistor string theory, and many more results ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)).
5. **National Medal of Science** (2002), MacArthur Fellowship (1982), and many other awards ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)).

## Context & Application

Witten's work bridges mathematics and physics to an extraordinary degree. His 1988 paper on topological Yang-Mills theory founded the subject of Witten-type TQFTs. His 1995 M-theory conjecture unified string theory and sparked the second superstring revolution. His contributions to the AdS/CFT correspondence (elaborating on Maldacena's conjecture) have shaped quantum gravity research. Witten has also contributed to the geometric Langlands program and Khovanov homology ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)): At the Strings '95 conference in 1995, Witten proposed that the five consistent superstring theories were limiting cases of a single theory in eleven dimensions, sparking the second superstring revolution.

**Example 2** ([edward-witten](/source/edward-witten/)): Witten's 1988 paper introduced topological quantum field theories of the Witten type, connecting quantum field theory computations to topological invariants of manifolds.
