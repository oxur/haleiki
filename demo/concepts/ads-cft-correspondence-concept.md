## Quick Definition

The AdS/CFT correspondence is a conjectured exact duality between a gravitational theory (string theory or M-theory) in anti-de Sitter space and a conformal field theory on its lower-dimensional boundary. It is the most successful realization of the holographic principle.

## Core Definition

The anti-de Sitter/conformal field theory correspondence is a conjectured relationship between two kinds of physical theories: gravitational theories in anti-de Sitter spaces (formulated via string theory or M-theory) and conformal field theories on the boundary. The duality provides a non-perturbative formulation of string theory with certain boundary conditions and is a strong-weak duality: strongly interacting particles in one description correspond to weakly interacting ones in the other ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)). First proposed by Juan Maldacena in late 1997 and elaborated by Gubser, Klebanov, Polyakov, and Witten, it has become the most cited paper in high-energy physics ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)).

## Prerequisites

- **String theory** -- AdS/CFT relates string theory to quantum field theory.
- **General relativity** -- anti-de Sitter space is a solution of Einstein's equations.

## Key Properties

1. **Holographic** -- a theory in $d+1$ dimensions is equivalent to one in $d$ dimensions on the boundary ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)).
2. **Strong-weak duality** -- strong coupling in one theory maps to weak coupling in the dual ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)).
3. **Exact equivalence** -- the predictions are quantitatively identical in both descriptions ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)).
4. **Dictionary** -- there is a translation between entities in the bulk gravitational theory and the boundary field theory ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)).
5. **Resolves black hole information paradox** -- in the AdS/CFT context, black hole evolution respects quantum unitarity ([string-theory](/source/string-theory/)).

## Context & Application

Maldacena's 1997 paper initiated the correspondence. The most prominent example is type IIB string theory on AdS5 x S5 dual to N=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory. Applications extend to nuclear physics (quark-gluon plasma viscosity), condensed matter physics (superfluid-insulator transitions), and quantum information theory ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([ads-cft-correspondence](/source/ads-cft-correspondence/)): The AdS5/CFT4 correspondence relates type IIB string theory on AdS5 x S5 to N=4 super Yang-Mills theory in four dimensions -- the best-studied and most famous example.

**Example 2** ([string-theory](/source/string-theory/)): Using AdS/CFT, the ratio of shear viscosity to entropy density of the quark-gluon plasma was predicted and confirmed at RHIC in 2008.
