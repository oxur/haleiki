## Quick Definition

The geodynamo is the mechanism by which convective motion of electrically conducting fluid in Earth's liquid outer core generates and sustains Earth's magnetic field. It is powered by both thermal and chemical convection.

## Core Definition

Dynamo theory sees eddy currents in the nickel-iron fluid of the outer core as the principal source of Earth's magnetic field ([earths-outer-core](/source/earths-outer-core/)). Earth's magnetic field is driven by thermal convection and also by chemical convection, the exclusion of light elements from the inner core, which float upward within the fluid outer core while denser elements sink. Carnot efficiencies suggest that compositional and thermal convection contribute about 80% and 20% respectively to the power of Earth's geodynamo ([earths-outer-core](/source/earths-outer-core/)). Convective heat transfer within the planet's high-temperature metallic core is theorized to sustain the geodynamo ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)).

## Prerequisites

- **Earth's outer core** — The liquid, electrically conducting layer where the geodynamo operates

## Key Properties

1. Generates Earth's magnetic field through convection of the liquid outer core
2. Powered primarily by chemical convection (~80%) and thermal convection (~20%)
3. The average magnetic field strength in the outer core is approximately 2.5 millitesla, 50 times stronger than at the surface
4. Essential for protecting life from interplanetary radiation and preventing atmospheric loss to the solar wind
5. The "new core paradox" questions how the geodynamo was sustained before inner core formation

## Context & Application

The magnetic field generated by the geodynamo is essential for life on Earth. Without it, the solar wind would strip away the atmosphere. Heat flow from the core, estimated at 5-15 TW, is necessary for maintaining the convecting outer core and the geodynamo ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)). One estimate suggests the core would not freeze for approximately 91 billion years ([internal-structure-of-earth](/source/internal-structure-of-earth/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([earths-outer-core](/source/earths-outer-core/)): As Earth's core cools, the liquid at the inner core boundary freezes, enriching the remaining liquid in light elements. This creates chemical convection currents thought to be the prime driver of the geodynamo.

## Relationships

- **Earth's outer core** is the fluid layer where the geodynamo operates
- **Earth's inner core** provides the compositional buoyancy that drives chemical convection
- **Mantle convection** controls the heat flow out of the core that sustains the geodynamo
