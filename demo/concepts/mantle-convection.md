## Quick Definition

Mantle convection is the slow, creeping circulation of Earth's solid mantle driven by internal heat, in which hotter, more buoyant material rises and cooler, denser material sinks. It is the dominant mechanism of heat transport from Earth's deep interior and the primary driver of plate tectonics.

## Core Definition

The mantle convects in response to heat escaping from Earth's interior, with hotter and more buoyant mantle rising and cooler, and therefore denser, mantle sinking ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)). Convection of the mantle propels the motion of the tectonic plates in the crust, driven by the decay of radioactive isotopes in Earth's crust and mantle combined with primordial heat from the planet's formation ([internal-structure-of-earth](/source/internal-structure-of-earth/)). Earth's internal heat flow to the surface is thought to be approximately 80% due to mantle convection ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)).

## Prerequisites

- **Mantle** — The layer in which convection occurs; understanding its composition and rheology is essential

## Key Properties

1. Responsible for approximately 80% of Earth's internal heat flow to the surface
2. Driven by two roughly equal heat sources: radiogenic heat and primordial heat
3. Causes hotter material to rise (upwelling) and cooler material to sink (downwelling)
4. Operates on geological timescales despite the mantle being predominantly solid
5. Directly drives the movement of lithospheric plates

## Context & Application

Mantle convection connects Earth's internal heat budget to surface geological processes. It drives plate tectonics, mountain building, rock metamorphism, and volcanism ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)). The controversy over the exact nature of mantle convection makes the linked evolution of Earth's heat budget and the dynamics of the mantle difficult to unravel.

## Examples

**Example 1** ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)): The highest heat flux values on the global map of Earth's heat flow occur at mid-ocean ridge spreading centers, which are zones of mantle upwelling.

## Relationships

- **Plate tectonics** is the surface expression of mantle convection
- **Radiogenic heat** and **primordial heat** are the two main energy sources powering convection
- **Mantle** is the medium in which convection occurs
