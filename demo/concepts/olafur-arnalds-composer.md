## Quick Definition

Olafur Arnalds (b. 1986) is an Icelandic composer and musician who blends classical and electronic music, known for his film scores, ambient compositions, and the Stratus generative music software.

## Core Definition

Olafur Arnalds is an Icelandic multi-instrumentalist, composer, and record producer, born in Mosfellsbaer, Iceland in 1986. His work spans film score, classical, experimental, electronica, and ambient music ([olafur-arnalds](/source/olafur-arnalds/)). Beginning from a background in hardcore punk and heavy metal, Arnalds transitioned to contemporary classical and electronic music, developing a distinctive style that combines strings and piano with electronic processing and generative software.
