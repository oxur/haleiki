## Quick Definition

The Nine Yanas are the Nyingma school's unique doxographic classification of the entire Buddhist path into nine vehicles (yanas), comprising three sutra vehicles, three outer tantric vehicles, and three inner tantric vehicles culminating in Dzogchen (Atiyoga).

## Core Definition

The doxography employed by the Nyingma tradition to categorize the whole of the Buddhist path is unique. Nyingmapas divide the Buddhist path into nine yanas ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)). The first two vehicles are seen as Hinayana, the third as Mahayana, and the remaining six as specifically Vajrayana ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)). Dudjom Jigdral Yeshe Dorje emphasized that the eight lower vehicles are intellectually fabricated and contrived, while Rongzom held that the views of sutra such as Madhyamaka were inferior to that of tantra ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).

## Prerequisites

- **Nyingma** -- the Nine Yanas is the Nyingma school's unique classification system; understanding the school provides necessary context.

## Key Properties

1. **Three sutra vehicles** -- Shravakayana (listeners), Pratyekabuddhayana (solitary buddhas), and Bodhisattvayana (Mahayana) ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).
2. **Three outer tantras** -- Kriya (action tantra), Carya/Ubhaya (conduct tantra), and Yogatantra (union tantra) ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).
3. **Three inner tantras** -- Mahayoga (great yoga), Anuyoga (subsequent yoga), and Atiyoga/Dzogchen (ultimate yoga/Great Perfection) ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).
4. **Dzogchen as pinnacle** -- Atiyoga (Dzogchen) is considered the highest vehicle, going beyond the methods of Highest Yoga Tantra as understood in other schools ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).
5. **Four continuum divisions** -- the nine vehicles are further categorized into Basis Continuum, Path Continuum, Result Continuum, and Method Continuum ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).

## Construction / Recognition

The nine yanas in order ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)):

1. **Shravakayana** -- the Vehicle of the Listeners
2. **Pratyekabuddhayana** -- the Vehicle of the Solitary Buddhas
3. **Bodhisattvayana** -- the Great Vehicle
4. **Kriya tantra** -- Tantra of Action
5. **Carya tantra** -- Tantra of Conduct
6. **Yoga tantra** -- Tantra of Union
7. **Mahayoga** -- Great Yoga
8. **Anuyoga** -- Subsequent Yoga
9. **Atiyoga (Dzogchen)** -- Ultimate Yoga / Great Perfection

## Context & Application

The nine yana classification is unique to the Nyingma school. Other Tibetan Buddhist schools (Sarma traditions) use a four-class tantra system (Kriya, Charya, Yoga, Anuttarayoga Tantra) where Anuttarayoga corresponds to Mahayoga in the Nyingma system, while Mahamudra teachings are said to lead to similar results as Dzogchen ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)). The eighteen major works of the inner tantra were transmitted and translated by Padmasambhava and Vimalamitra, with Samantabhadra as the origin ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)): The Nyingma Gyubum (Hundred Thousand Tantras of the Ancient School) organizes the Nyingma esoteric corpus according to the inner tantra classification: 10 volumes of Atiyoga (Dzogchen), 3 volumes of Anuyoga, 6 volumes of tantra section of Mahayoga, and 13 volumes of sadhana section of Mahayoga.
