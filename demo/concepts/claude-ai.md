## Quick Definition

Claude is a series of large language models developed by Anthropic, first released in 2023, trained using "constitutional AI" to improve ethical and legal compliance. Each generation is typically released in three sizes: Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus.

## Core Definition

Claude is a series of large language models developed by Anthropic and first released in 2023 ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)). Claude is trained using "constitutional AI," a technique developed by Anthropic to improve ethical and legal compliance (AI alignment) ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)). The name Claude has been described both as a tribute to Claude Shannon, who pioneered information theory, and as a friendly counterpart to virtual assistants like Alexa and Siri ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)). Claude models are generative pre-trained transformers that have been trained to predict the next word in large amounts of text, then fine-tuned using reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and constitutional AI ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)).

## Key Properties

1. **Three-tier model family** -- each generation is released in three sizes: Haiku (smallest), Sonnet (medium), and Opus (largest) ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)).
2. **Constitutional AI training** -- uses a constitutional AI approach for alignment rather than pure RLHF ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)).
3. **Multi-modal capabilities** -- can process different types of data including images, videos, sound, and text ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)).
4. **Ethical stance** -- Anthropic refused to remove contractual prohibitions on use for mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)).
5. **Software development use** -- used for software development via Claude Code ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)).

## Context & Application

Claude was first released in March 2023. US federal agencies started phasing out Claude after Anthropic refused to allow its use for mass surveillance and autonomous weapons, leading to the Department of Defense designating Anthropic a "supply chain risk" ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)). A federal judge issued a temporary injunction against this designation in March 2026 ([claude-language-model](/source/claude-language-model/)).
