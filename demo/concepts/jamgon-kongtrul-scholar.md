## Quick Definition

Jamgon Kongtrul Lodro Thaye (1813-1899) was a Tibetan Buddhist scholar, polymath, and co-founder of the Rime (non-sectarian) movement who composed over 90 volumes of Buddhist writing, including the Five Great Treasuries, preserving endangered teachings from multiple schools.

## Core Definition

Jamgon Kongtrul was a Tibetan Buddhist scholar, poet, artist, physician, terton, and polymath credited as one of the founders of the Rime movement. He composed over 90 volumes of Buddhist writing, including his magnum opus, The Treasury of Knowledge ([jamgon-kongtrul](/source/jamgon-kongtrul/)). Working with Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo, Chogyur Lingpa, and Mipham Gyatso, he compiled, exchanged, and revived the teachings of the Sakya, Kagyu, and Nyingma, including many near-extinct teachings ([jamgon-kongtrul](/source/jamgon-kongtrul/)). The Rinchen Terdzod, assembled by Kongtrul at the behest of Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo, is the most important collection of terma treasure texts for Nyingmapas ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)).

## Key Properties

1. **Five Great Treasuries** -- his most significant compilation work, including the Treasury of Knowledge and the Rinchen Terdzod ([jamgon-kongtrul](/source/jamgon-kongtrul/)).
2. **Rime co-founder** -- co-founded the non-sectarian movement with Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo ([jamgon-kongtrul](/source/jamgon-kongtrul/)).
3. **Prolific author** -- composed over 90 volumes of Buddhist writing ([jamgon-kongtrul](/source/jamgon-kongtrul/)).
4. **Shentong Madhyamaka** -- promoted Shentong view as a valid Madhyamaka position ([jamgon-kongtrul](/source/jamgon-kongtrul/)).
5. **Multi-lineage** -- studied and received empowerments from more than sixty masters across all schools ([jamgon-kongtrul](/source/jamgon-kongtrul/)).
