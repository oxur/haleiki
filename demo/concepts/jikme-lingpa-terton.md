## Quick Definition

Jikme Lingpa (1730-1798), also known as "Khyentse Ozer" ("Rays of Compassion and Wisdom"), was a great Nyingma scholar and visionary who discovered the Longchen Nyingtik cycle of Dzogchen teachings through visions of Longchenpa, making it one of the most widely practiced Nyingma traditions.

## Core Definition

Jikme Lingpa is regarded as one of the most important figures in the Nyingma lineage. He discovered the Longchen Nyingtik cycle of teachings and practice through a series of visions from Longchenpa ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)). He discovered the Longchen Nyingtik teachings as mind terma at the age of twenty-eight in 1757 ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/), [longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)). With the patronage of the Derge royal family, he published the compilation of Nyingma tantras known as the Nyingma Gyubum and composed a catalogue to accompany it ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)).

## Key Properties

1. **Revealer of Longchen Nyingtik** -- discovered this widely practiced Dzogchen cycle as mind terma ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)).
2. **Three visions of Longchenpa** -- received the blessing and transmission of Longchenpa's wisdom through three pure visions during retreat at Chimpu near Samye ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)).
3. **Incarnation of Trisong Detsen and Vimalamitra** -- was a reincarnation of both figures, uniting the two major Nyingtik lineages ([longchen-nyingtik](/source/longchen-nyingtik/)).
4. **Published Nyingma Gyubum** -- compiled and published the Nyingma tantras collection with Derge royal patronage ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)).
5. **Author of Treasury of Precious Qualities** -- his most well-known original composition ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)).

## Context & Application

Jikme Lingpa's Longchen Nyingthig is described as "the greatest treasure finder of the eighteenth century" and his systematization became one of the most widely used Nyingma Dzogchen teachings ([nyingma](/source/nyingma/)). He believed that students of superior faculties were extremely rare and that for most people, a gradual path of training is needed to reach realization ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([jikme-lingpa](/source/jikme-lingpa/)): Jikme Lingpa discovered the Longchen Nyingtik at Boudhanath Stupa in a visionary experience where wisdom dakinis gave him a casket containing five yellow scrolls and seven crystal beads.
