## Quick Definition

Angiosperms (flowering plants) are the most diverse group of land plants, distinguished by having flowers, fruits that enclose seeds, and xylem with vessel elements. They comprise approximately 300,000 known species across 64 orders and 416 families.

## Core Definition

Flowering plants are plants that bear flowers and fruits, forming the clade Angiospermae. The term "angiosperm" derives from Greek words meaning "container" and "seed," referring to seeds enclosed within a fruit ([flowering-plant](/source/flowering-plant/)). Angiosperms are distinguished from gymnosperms by having flowers, xylem consisting of vessel elements instead of tracheids, endosperm within their seeds, and fruits that completely envelop the seeds. They diverged from gymnosperms before the end of the Carboniferous period, over 300 million years ago, and diversified explosively during the Cretaceous ([flowering-plant](/source/flowering-plant/)).

## Key Properties

1. Bear flowers (unique reproductive organs among seed plants)
2. Seeds enclosed within fruits (carpels)
3. Xylem made of vessel elements (more efficient than gymnosperm tracheids)
4. Endosperm forms after fertilization to nourish the embryo
5. Reduced gametophytes (smaller than gymnosperm gametophytes)
6. Most diverse land plant group: ~300,000 species, 64 orders, 416 families
7. Include forbs, grasses, most broad-leaved trees, shrubs, vines, and most aquatic plants
8. Approximately 99% are photosynthetic autotrophs

## Context & Application

Agriculture is almost entirely dependent on angiosperms. Rice, maize, and wheat (all grasses from family Poaceae) provide half of the world's staple calorie intake ([flowering-plant](/source/flowering-plant/)). The largest angiosperms are Eucalyptus gum trees of Australia, reaching almost 100 m in height; the smallest are Wolffia duckweeds, less than 2 mm across. Many flowering plants are threatened by habitat destruction and climate change.

## Relationships

- **Gymnosperm** contrasts with angiosperms as the other major seed plant clade, lacking flowers and enclosed seeds
- **Pollination** is the reproductive mechanism enabling angiosperm diversification
- **Eucalyptus** is a major angiosperm genus with over 700 species
- **Mycorrhiza** associations are present in most angiosperm species

## Common Confusions

- **Flowering plants are not all colorful**: Many angiosperms, like grasses and oaks, have inconspicuous wind-pollinated flowers
- **Angiosperm vs. plant**: Not all plants are angiosperms; ferns, mosses, and conifers are plants but not angiosperms
