## Quick Definition

Max Richter (b. 1966) is a German-born British composer working at the intersection of contemporary classical music, ambient, and post-minimalism. His works include the 8-hour Sleep project and Recomposed by Max Richter: Vivaldi -- The Four Seasons.

## Core Definition

Max Richter is a contemporary classical composer born in Hamelin, Germany, in 1966. He studied at the University of Edinburgh and the Royal Academy of Music. His work spans contemporary classical, ambient, minimalism, and post-minimalism ([max-richter](/source/max-richter/)). Richter's compositional approach often combines classical instrumentation with electronic elements, creating a distinctively modern sound that bridges classical and popular audiences.

## Context & Application

Richter is part of a generation of composers who have expanded the audience for classical music by incorporating elements of electronic and ambient music. His approach reflects the post-minimalist tradition descended from Glass and Part, while engaging with contemporary technology and media ([max-richter](/source/max-richter/)).
