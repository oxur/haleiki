## Quick Definition

An eye is a sensory organ that detects light and converts it into electrochemical impulses in neurons, enabling an organism to perceive visual information. Eyes with resolving power exist in ten fundamentally different forms, classified as compound or non-compound.

## Core Definition

An eye is a sensory organ that allows an organism to perceive visual information. It detects light and converts it into electrochemical impulses in neurons, forming part of an organism's visual system ([eye](/source/eye/)). In higher organisms, the eye is a complex optical system that collects light, regulates its intensity through a diaphragm (iris), focuses it through adjustable lenses to form an image, converts this image into electrical signals, and transmits these to the brain through the optic nerve to the visual cortex ([eye](/source/eye/)). The first proto-eyes evolved about 600 million years ago around the time of the Cambrian explosion.

## Key Properties

1. Ten fundamentally different eye layouts have evolved
2. Two major categories: compound eyes (multiple lenses) and simple/camera-type eyes (single lens)
3. Compound eyes are common in insects and crustaceans; camera eyes in mammals
4. Vertebrate eyes use cone cells (color) and rod cells (low-light) in the retina
5. Proto-eyes first evolved ~600 million years ago
6. More advanced eyes have evolved in 96% of animal species
7. Some eyes serve non-visual functions (circadian rhythm regulation, pupillary reflex)

## Context & Application

Eyes are the primary sensory organ for visual perception, which is arguably the dominant human sense. The evolution of eyes in ten different forms demonstrates convergent evolution driven by the selective advantage of detecting light. The mantis shrimp has the world's most complex color vision system with detailed hyperspectral capability ([eye](/source/eye/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([eye](/source/eye/)): Jumping spiders have one pair of large simple eyes for high-resolution forward vision, augmented by smaller eyes for peripheral vision.

**Example 2** ([eye](/source/eye/)): Trilobites had unique compound eyes with lenses made of clear calcite crystals, unlike the soft eyes of other arthropods.
