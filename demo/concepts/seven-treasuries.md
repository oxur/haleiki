## Quick Definition

The Seven Treasuries (Tibetan: mdzod bdun, Dzo Dun) are the most famous works of Longchenpa, a collection of seven treatises that systematically elucidate the seventeen main tantras of Dzogchen and the teachings of all nine yanas of the Nyingma tradition.

## Core Definition

Longchenpa systematized the Nyingma teachings in his Seven Treasuries ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)). As Nyoshul Khen Rinpoche explains: "Kunkhyen Longchenpa's Seven Treasuries were written to elucidate the extraordinarily profound meaning of the seventeen main tantras of Dzogpachenpo as well as the teachings of all nine yanas" ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)). The Seven Treasuries, along with the Three Trilogies, are Longchenpa's most well-known works, representing the most comprehensive systematization of Nyingma Dzogchen teachings.

## Prerequisites

- **Longchenpa** -- the Seven Treasuries are Longchenpa's principal works; understanding his role as a systematizer is essential context.
- **Dzogchen** -- the Seven Treasuries elucidate the tantras of Dzogchen; familiarity with Dzogchen as a teaching tradition is necessary to understand their content and significance.

## Key Properties

1. **Seven distinct treasuries** -- each addresses different aspects of Buddhist philosophy and Dzogchen practice.
2. **Comprehensive systematization** -- together they elucidate the seventeen main tantras of Dzogchen and all nine yanas.
3. **Theory and practice** -- while the Seven Treasuries provide the theoretical framework, the Nyingtik Yabshi provides the practice aspect ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)).

## Construction / Recognition

The seven works comprising the collection are ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)):

1. **Wish Fulfilling Treasury** (Yishyin Dzo)
2. **Treasury of Pith Instructions** (Mengak Dzo)
3. **Treasury of Dharmadhatu** (Choying Dzo)
4. **Treasury of Philosophical Tenets** (Drubta Dzo)
5. **Treasury of the Supreme Vehicle** (Tekchok Dzo)
6. **Treasury of Word and Meaning** (Tsik Don Dzo)
7. **Treasury of the Natural State** (Neluk Dzo)

## Context & Application

Many of Longchenpa's writings were composed at Gangri Thokar ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)). The Seven Treasuries stand alongside the Three Trilogies (Trilogy of Dispelling Darkness, Trilogy of Finding Comfort and Ease, Trilogy of Natural Freedom) as Longchenpa's principal contributions. The Seven Treasuries provide the doctrinal framework while the Nyingtik Yabshi (a thirteen-volume collection) provides the practice aspect, synthesizing the Vima Nyingtik of Vimalamitra and the Khandro Nyingtik of Guru Rinpoche ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([longchenpa](/source/longchenpa/)): The Treasury of Dharmadhatu (Choying Dzo) addresses the expanse of reality, reflecting its subject in Longchenpa's use of the name "Longchen Rabjam" in its colophon -- a name he reserved for "writings in which the inconceivable nature is taught in detail."
