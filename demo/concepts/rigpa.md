## Quick Definition

Rigpa (Sanskrit: vidya, "knowledge") is the central concept in Dzogchen, referring to the direct, non-conceptual knowledge of the fundamental nature of mind -- an awareness that is knowing, clear, and unchanging.

## Core Definition

Rigpa is a central concept in Dzogchen. According to a text from the Heart Essence of Vimalamitra: "vidya is knowing, clear, and unchanging." Simply put, vidya means unconfused knowledge of the basis that is its own state ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)). The goal of Dzogchen is the direct experience of this basis, called rigpa ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)). According to Sam van Schaik, rigpa and the ground (gzhi) represent the "gnoseological and ontological aspects of the nirvanic state" respectively ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).

## Prerequisites

- **Dzogchen** -- rigpa is the central concept of the Dzogchen tradition; understanding the Dzogchen framework of base, path, and fruit is necessary context.

## Key Properties

1. **Non-conceptual knowledge** -- rigpa is direct, non-dual, non-conceptual knowledge of the pure nature of mind ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).
2. **Knowing, clear, unchanging** -- these three qualities define rigpa according to the Vima Nyingtik tradition ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).
3. **Opposite of ma rigpa** -- ma rigpa (avidya) is ignorance or unawareness, the failure to recognize the nature of the basis ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).
4. **Beyond effort** -- rigpa is "perfect wisdom in the realm beyond effort" according to the Mirror of the Heart of Vajrasattva ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).

## Context & Application

In Dzogchen practice, the path consists of the integration of all experiences in one's life with the state of rigpa. All experiences are self-liberated through this integration. The practitioner's development moves through three stages of liberation capacity: cherdrol (liberation through observation), shardrol (liberation upon arising), and rangdrol (instantaneous self-liberation) ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)): Garab Dorje's first statement of the three vital points is "Introducing directly the face of rigpa itself" -- the direct introduction to the practitioner's own intrinsic awareness by an empowering master.

**Example 2** ([dzogchen](/source/dzogchen/)): The "Empowerment of Awareness" (rigpay sall wahng) is a particular lineage of empowerment propagated by Jigme Lingpa, consisting of the direct introduction of the student to the intrinsic nature of their own mind-essence, rigpa.

## Relationships

- **Prerequisite: dzogchen** -- rigpa is the central concept of the Dzogchen tradition.
- **Related to nyingma** -- rigpa is the nature of mind recognized in Nyingma Dzogchen practice.
- **Related to buddha-nature** -- rigpa relates to the tathagata-garbha or buddha-nature doctrine.
- **Contrasts with ma-rigpa** -- ma rigpa (ignorance) is the opposite of rigpa, the failure to recognize the nature of the basis.
