## Quick Definition

Primordial heat is the residual thermal energy from Earth's original formation, still being lost as the planet continues to cool. It accounts for roughly half of Earth's total internal heat output.

## Core Definition

Primordial heat is the heat lost by the Earth as it continues to cool from its original formation, in contrast to its still actively-produced radiogenic heat ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)). The Earth core's heat flow, estimated at 5-15 TW, is thought to be due primarily to primordial heat. Estimates of mantle primordial heat loss range between 7 and 15 TW. Primordial heat energy comes from the potential energy released by collapsing a large amount of matter into a gravity well, and the kinetic energy of accreted matter ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)).

## Prerequisites

- **Mantle** — Primordial heat is stored in and flows through Earth's mantle and core

## Key Properties

1. Residual heat from Earth's formation approximately 4.6 billion years ago
2. Core heat flow estimated at 5-15 TW
3. Mantle primordial heat loss estimated at 7-15 TW
4. Sources: gravitational potential energy from accretion and kinetic energy of accreted matter
5. Essential for maintaining the geodynamo and Earth's magnetic field

## Context & Application

Heat flow from the core is necessary for maintaining the convecting outer core and the geodynamo that generates Earth's magnetic field. Therefore, primordial heat from the core enabled Earth's atmosphere and helped retain liquid water ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([earths-internal-heat-budget](/source/earths-internal-heat-budget/)): The early formation of Earth's dense core caused superheating and rapid heat loss; the rate slowed once the mantle solidified.

## Relationships

- **Radiogenic heat** is the other major heat source, actively produced rather than residual
- **Geodynamo** depends on heat flowing from the core, which is primarily primordial
- **Mantle convection** is sustained by the combination of primordial and radiogenic heat
