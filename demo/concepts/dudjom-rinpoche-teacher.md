## Quick Definition

Dudjom Rinpoche Jigdral Yeshe Dorje (1904-1987) was a Nyingma master, terton, prolific author, and the first appointed head of the Nyingma school in exile. He was recognized as the incarnation of Dudjom Lingpa and is considered a direct incarnation of Padmasambhava.

## Core Definition

Dudjom Rinpoche was a Nyingma householder, a yogi, and a Vajrayana and Dzogchen master. He was recognized as the incarnation of Terton Dudjom Lingpa (1835-1904), a renowned treasure revealer, and was considered a direct incarnation of both Padmasambhava and Dudjom Lingpa ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)). Unique in having received the transmission of all the existing teachings of the Nyingma tradition, he was especially renowned as a great terton and a leading exponent of Dzogchen ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)). The 14th Dalai Lama appointed Dudjom Rinpoche as the first head of the Nyingma school in the early 1960s to help preserve the Vajrayana vehicle in exile ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)).

## Key Properties

1. **First Nyingma head** -- appointed by the 14th Dalai Lama as head of the Nyingma school in exile from 1960 until his death in 1987 ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)).
2. **Complete Nyingma transmission holder** -- unique in having received the transmission of all existing Nyingma teachings ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)).
3. **Prolific author** -- wrote The Nyingma School of Tibetan Buddhism: Its Fundamentals and History, political history of Tibet, and twenty-five volumes of collected works ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)).
4. **Terton** -- his terma texts are widely taught and practiced, revealing concealed treasures particularly related to Vajrakila practice ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)).
5. **Preservation work** -- helped recover all texts of the Nyingma Kama and Terma lineages, transferring them out of Tibet to save the canon during the Chinese invasion ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)).

## Context & Application

Dudjom Rinpoche was born in Pemako, a sacred hidden land in southern Tibet, and studied at great monastic universities including Mindrolling, Dorje Drak, Kathok, and Dzogchen Monastery ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)). After exile, he established vital communities of practitioners in India and Nepal and built monasteries across multiple countries. His activities brought Vajrayana and Nyingma teachings to the Western world. He was revered as the "Greatest Terton of Our Time" ([dudjom-rinpoche](/source/dudjom-rinpoche/)).
