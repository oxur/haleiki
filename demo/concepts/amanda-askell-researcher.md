## Quick Definition

Amanda Askell is a philosopher and AI researcher at Anthropic, recognized in Time 100 AI (2024), who leads work on Claude's character training and AI alignment. Her academic background spans analytic philosophy, with doctoral work on infinite ethics under David Chalmers.

## Core Definition

Amanda Askell is a philosopher who works at Anthropic on AI alignment and the character training of the Claude language model family ([amanda-askell](/source/amanda-askell/)). She received her PhD from New York University in 2018 with a thesis on "Pareto Principles in Infinite Ethics," supervised by Cian Dorr, David Chalmers, and Shelly Kagan ([amanda-askell](/source/amanda-askell/)).
