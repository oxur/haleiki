## Quick Definition

Subduction is the geological process at convergent plate boundaries where one tectonic plate descends beneath another into the mantle. It is the primary mechanism by which oceanic lithosphere is recycled and continental crust is generated.

## Core Definition

At convergent plate boundaries, lithosphere is consumed by the process of subduction, where an ocean plate sinks back into the mantle ([tectonics](/source/tectonics/)). Opposite a mid-ocean spreading center, there is usually a subduction zone: a trench where old oceanic crust is destroyed. This constant process of creating new ocean crust at ridges and destroying old crust at trenches means that the oldest ocean crust on Earth today is only about 200 million years old ([earths-crust](/source/earths-crust/)). Partial melting of the mantle at subduction zones produces continental crust.

## Prerequisites

- **Plate tectonics** — Subduction occurs at convergent plate boundaries, requiring understanding of plate motion

## Key Properties

1. Occurs at convergent plate boundaries
2. One plate (typically oceanic) descends beneath another into the mantle
3. Responsible for destruction of oceanic lithosphere
4. Generates continental crust through partial melting of the mantle
5. Associated with major earthquakes and volcanic activity
6. Limits the maximum age of oceanic crust to approximately 200 million years

## Context & Application

Subduction zones are responsible for most of the world's major earthquakes and are the sites of significant volcanic activity. Formation of new continental crust is linked to periods of intense orogeny at subduction zones ([earths-crust](/source/earths-crust/)). Thrust (contractional) tectonics at convergent boundaries results in the shortening and thickening of the crust ([tectonics](/source/tectonics/)).

## Examples

**Example 1** ([earths-crust](/source/earths-crust/)): Secondary oceanic crust forms at mid-ocean spreading centers, while old crust is destroyed at subduction zones, so the oldest ocean crust is only about 200 million years old.

- **Oceanic crust** is the primary material consumed at subduction zones
